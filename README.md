# Hi, I'm Tara! :-)

I'm a doctoral candidate and research assistant in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at CMU, working with Dr. Ismaila Dabo. My research combines first-principles simulation and machine learning to understand and design materials for energy applications.

### What I Do
- **Materials Modeling** — First-principles calculations to uncover structure--property relationships in materials.
- **Phonons & Disorder** — Studying vibrational properties and thermal transport in high-entropy and disordered oxides.
- **ML for Materials Design** — Implementing deep learning and MLIPs to accelerate materials discovery and property prediction.
- **Sustainable Energy** — Designing materials with tailored properties for thermoelectric and refrigeration applications.
